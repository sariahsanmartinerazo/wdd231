Traveling with purpose

Website Planning Document

Site Name

This name reflects the idea of traveling with an academic and professional purpose. The website helps chemical engineers explore master's degree opportunities in Mexico and abroad while considering their academic and career goals.

Site Purpose

Traveling with Purpose will provide information about master's degree programs available to chemical engineers in Mexico and abroad. The website will help Mexican chemical engineers explore universities and institutions, compare academic programs, review admission requirements, and discover opportunities to continue their professional and academic development.

Scenarios

  • What master's degree programs are available for chemical engineers in Mexico?
  • what are the admission requirements for a master's degree program abroad, and what university offers it?
  • Which master's degree programs are related to my professional interests and career goals?
